# Late Fees of the Dead: art prompts for stages 2 to 10

Paste these into ChatGPT image generation. Every stage and monster already works with drawn stand-ins, so you can add art in any order. Send the images back and I'll convert them, cut out the monsters and register them; each one replaces its stand-in as soon as it's in.

## Part 1: Background panoramas (27 images, 3 per stage)

Each stage scrolls through three areas. Each area needs one wide panorama, which the game tiles sideways as the camera moves.

**How to use:** attach one of the existing backdrops (`art/bg-suburb.webp`, `bg-mall.webp` or `bg-lot.webp`) as a style reference. Paste the style block, then one scene line. Save the result under the file name shown.

**Style block (paste first, every time):**

> Match the art style of the attached reference exactly: thick black ink outlines, grainy 90s comic-book / VHS-cover texture, warm campy B-movie colors, same level of detail. Make a wide side-scrolling video game background, landscape 3:1 (about 1944x648), side-on flat view like a beat-'em-up stage. The walkable floor starts at about 62% down the image and runs to the bottom edge, so keep that bottom strip clear, plain, flat floor or ground with no objects, people or props standing in it. Everything interesting goes in the top 62%. No characters, no monsters, no people, no text other than signs described below. The left and right edges should line up so the image can tile side by side.
