# The Last Video Store: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information The Last Video Store (the "Game") uses, where it goes and what you can do about it. It covers the browser version and, when it is released, the Steam version. "We" means Our Bad Entertainment.

## 1. The short version
- The Game has no accounts, sign-ups, ads, analytics or tracking cookies.
- Your progress and settings stay on your own device.
- When you play online, our room server briefly handles your connection and your button presses to run the game, then forgets them when the room closes.
- We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never have.

## 2. What stays on your device
The Game saves the following in your browser's local storage (or, in the Steam version, in a save file on your computer):
- your gear, chosen hero, high score and unlocked stages;
- your settings: volume, DJ June, controls, console skin, stick type, difficulty and region.

This information is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the save file, erases it.

## 3. What we receive when you play online
When you host or join an online room, your device connects to our room server, which runs on Cloudflare. To run the room, the server receives:
- **your IP address and basic connection details**, which every internet connection needs;
- **your button presses** while you play;
- **your hero, gear stats and outfit look**, so other players see you correctly;
- **your connection speed (ping)**, which is shown to the other players in the room as a coloured dot;
- **a random rejoin code** made for that room, so you can reconnect if your connection drops.

The server does not receive your name, email or any account details. It shows players as P1 to P4.

**How long we keep it:** this information is held in memory only while the room is open and is discarded when the room closes. The server stores nothing about you after that; the only thing it saves is the time a room was created, which contains no personal information.

## 4. Services that handle data for us
- **Cloudflare** runs our room servers. It processes network traffic, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service, under its own privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy).
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the browser version. Like any website host, it receives your IP address when you load the Game, under GitHub's privacy statement (docs.github.com/site-policy).
- **Steam** (Valve) handles purchases, your Steam account and, if you use it, Steam Cloud backups of your save file, under Valve's privacy policy (store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement). We do not receive your payment details.

The Game's fonts, art and music are part of the Game itself and load from the same place as the Game, so playing does not contact any other company.

## 5. What we do not do
- We do not sell or "share" personal information, as those words are used in California law, and we do not use it for advertising.
- We do not track you across other websites or apps, and we do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals because there is no tracking to stop.
- We do not use your information to train artificial intelligence.

## 6. Children
The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the Game has no accounts and keeps progress on your device, it does not ask anyone for personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to know what personal information we have about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Because we keep no personal information after a room closes, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete, but you are welcome to ask. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, contact us as described below.

## 8. Security
Connections to our room server are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep as little information as possible, for as short a time as possible.

## 9. Changes to this policy
If we change what information the Game uses (for example, if we add online saves tied to your Steam account), we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

## 10. Contact
Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to Our Bad Entertainment at ourbadentertainment.com.
